Backtest only -- this tool never places an order, paper or live; it replays historical (or synthetic demo) 1-minute candles against a fixed mechanical rule set and shows you what would have happened, including modeled tick size, spread, slippage, and commission. The 8:00-10:00am ET window is largely pre-market for US equities (regular session opens 9:30am ET) -- liquidity is thinner and spreads wider there than midday, which is why the default cost assumptions are more conservative than a typical regular-hours estimate; adjust them to match your own broker/instrument. Past performance, simulated or real, does not guarantee future results. Not financial advice. See the "Range Breakout Web App" section of the repo README for the exact mechanical rules, including the handful of judgment calls made to turn a natural-language strategy into deterministic code.
