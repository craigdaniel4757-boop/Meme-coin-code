01 · Practice area

Business Valuations

As Chartered Business Valuators (CBVs), we provide business valuations for many purposes, including:

  • Shareholder disputes
  • Shareholder transactions
  • Succession planning
  • Estate planning
  • Income tax planning, including corporate reorganizations
  • Mergers and acquisitions
  • Family law matters
  • Expropriation
  • Purchase price allocations
  • Business agreements (shareholder, partnership, joint venture)
  • Professional practices
  • Trust interests
  • Employee stock options
  • Critiques of reports prepared by others

Types of valuation reports

We prepare the following types of valuation reports:

Comprehensive Valuation Report

Highest assurance

Estimate Valuation Report

Moderate assurance

Calculation Valuation Report

Lowest assurance

The conclusions differ by the level of assurance provided and the extent of analysis, investigation and corroboration performed: the breadth of work is the same, only the depth differs. Refer to our Frequently Asked Questions for further information on the types of reports.

03 · Practice area

Litigation Support

As litigation consultants, we provide assistance to legal counsel in financial matters regarding their clients. Our litigation support services include:

  1. Business valuations
  2. Pre-trial preparation (financial matters)
  3. Preparation of financial questions for opposing experts
  4. Clarification of financial issues for legal counsel
  5. Review of valuation reports prepared by others
  6. Assistance to legal counsel in mediations and arbitrations
  7. Expert witness testimony on financial matters

04 · Practice area

Family Law Matters

As a subset of our litigation support services, we have particular expertise in family law financial matters. Our family law services include:

  1. Business valuations
  2. Income calculations for child and spousal support
  3. Calculations of net family property
  4. Tracing of funds for excluded property
  5. Assistance in settlements between spouses
  6. Pre-trial preparation (financial matters)
  7. Preparation of financial questions for opposing experts
  8. Review of valuation reports prepared by others
  9. Assistance to legal counsel in mediations and arbitrations
  10. Expert witness testimony on financial matters
