02 · Confidentiality

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

ETVI is committed to client confidentiality and to protecting the personal information entrusted to us by our clients and by visitors to this website.

This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard personal information, in keeping with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Information we collect

We collect only the personal information we need to respond to enquiries and to provide our services. This may include:

  • your name and contact details, and the content of any message you send us by telephone, email or through this website; and
  • business and financial information that you or your advisors provide to us in the course of an engagement.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiries and communicate with you.
  • To plan, perform and manage the engagements you ask us to carry out.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

Confidentiality and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Client information is held in confidence and is disclosed only with your consent, where required by law, regulation or professional standards, or to service providers who support our operations and are required to protect it.

This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Information you submit through our contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry. Our contact page includes an embedded Google Map; when it loads, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, as described in Google’s own privacy policy. Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Safeguards and retention

We protect personal information with physical, organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity, and we retain it only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law and professional standards.

Access and questions
