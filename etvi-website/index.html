Our process

A rigorous, transparent valuation process.

A business valuation prepared by a CBV typically involves a number of steps to fulfill the professional standards of the Canadian Institute of Chartered Business Valuators (CICBV).

  1. 01

    Engage

    1. aInitial interview with the business owner and/or management
    2. bEngagement letter signed by the client
  2. 02

    Analyze

    1. cCompilation of information for the assignment
    2. dInitial review and analysis of the information
    3. eResearch of the industry, transactions, etc. as appropriate
  3. 03

    Review

    1. fFollow-up questions and possibly additional interviews
    2. gPreparation of a draft report
    3. hComments and discussion with the business owner/management
  4. 04

    Finalize

    1. iRepresentation letter signed by the client
    2. jRelease of final report
Typically 3 to 6 weeks. Timing depends on the type of report, the size and complexity of the business, and the availability of information. Valuation FAQs
